Scaler Academy — Refund Policy (Effective April 2026)

Clause 1. Cooling-off period. A learner may request a full refund within 7 calendar days of their first live class, provided they have attended no more than two live sessions. Under this clause, 100% of the program fee is refunded, excluding the one-time non-refundable registration fee of Rs. 5,000.

Clause 2. Partial refund window. A refund request filed after the 7-day cooling-off period but within 15 calendar days of the first live class is eligible for a 50% refund of the program fee. The registration fee of Rs. 5,000 remains non-refundable.

Clause 3. No refund after 15 days. No refund is available once 15 calendar days have elapsed from the date of the first live class, except under the medical-withdrawal provision in Clause 4.

Clause 4. Medical withdrawal. A learner who must withdraw due to a documented medical emergency may apply for a prorated refund at any time. The request must be filed within 30 days of the incident and accompanied by a medical certificate. Prorated refunds deduct the cost of completed modules.

Clause 5. Scholarship and discounts. If a learner received a merit scholarship or promotional discount, the refund is calculated on the net fee actually paid, not the sticker price.

Clause 6. EMI-financed enrolments. For learners paying via a no-cost EMI plan through a financing partner, an approved refund cancels the remaining EMI schedule. Interest already borne by Scaler is not passed on to the learner.

Clause 7. Processing time. Approved refunds are processed within 21 business days to the original payment method. Learners are notified by email at each stage.

Clause 8. Deferral alternative. In place of a refund, a learner may defer their enrolment once to a later cohort at no additional cost, provided the deferral is requested before 50% of the program duration has elapsed.
