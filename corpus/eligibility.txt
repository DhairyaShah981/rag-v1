Scaler — Admissions and Eligibility Criteria (2026 Cohorts)

Clause 1. Educational qualification. Applicants must hold a bachelor's degree in any discipline with an aggregate of at least 50%. Final-year students awaiting their degree are eligible to apply and may enrol conditionally, subject to submitting their degree certificate within six months.

Clause 2. Coding experience. Scaler Academy (Software Development track) requires a minimum of 6 months of prior programming experience in any language. The Data Science and Machine Learning program requires familiarity with basic Python and school-level mathematics; no professional experience is mandatory.

Clause 3. Entrance assessment. Every applicant must clear the Scaler Skills Test (SST), a 90-minute online assessment covering logical reasoning and basic programming. A score of 60% or higher is required to proceed to enrolment. The SST may be reattempted once after a 14-day cooling period.

Clause 4. Age requirement. Applicants must be at least 18 years of age at the time of enrolment. There is no upper age limit.

Clause 5. Working professionals. The programs are designed for working professionals. Live classes are scheduled on weekends and weekday evenings so that learners need not leave their current jobs.

Clause 6. English proficiency. All instruction is in English. Applicants must be comfortable reading technical material and participating in discussions in English. No formal English test is required.

Clause 7. Placement eligibility. Access to the placement and career services is contingent on (a) completing the program with at least 80% attendance, (b) clearing all module assessments, and (c) completing the capstone project. Learners who withdraw or defer forfeit placement eligibility for that cohort.
